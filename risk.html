02 / Security Impact

Risk Analysis

When cryptography is weak or implemented incorrectly, the damage can go far beyond the original mistake.

01
🔓

Data Exposure

Weak encryption or poor key management can expose personal, financial or business information.

02
👤

Account Theft

Poor password hashing or exposed credentials can make it easier for attackers to access accounts.

03
💳

Financial Loss

Exposed payment information or compromised accounts can lead to financial damage.

04
⚠️

Loss of Trust

A security incident can damage the trust users have in an application or organization.

What increases the risk?

Old Algorithms

Older algorithms may no longer provide enough protection against modern attacks.

Weak Password Storage

Storing passwords incorrectly can make stolen databases much more dangerous.

Exposed Keys

Secret keys should not be stored in public repositories or exposed systems.

Important: Encryption by itself does not guarantee security. The algorithm, implementation, keys and configuration all matter.
